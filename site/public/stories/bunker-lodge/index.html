Looks like a ski lodge. Isn't. One drive in, ending at a door set into the mountain.

Inside, a stone fire, sheepskins, and the peaks in every window. At night, the lights wake as you walk up.

That door is a vault door, the walls are Faraday-lined, and a spring fills the cistern. Lodge or canyon: where do you ride out the winter?
