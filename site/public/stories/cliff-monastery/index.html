The story

300 steps. Worth every one.*

Three hundred steps. Worth every one. Park at the top, then one stone stair down the cliff, with a lift for the groceries.

Whitewashed vaults, a fire, and the gorge through an arched window. At night, the lights wake down the stair.

The old walls are Faraday-lined, and the storerooms are cut into the cliff. Monastery or sea stack: could you live out here?
